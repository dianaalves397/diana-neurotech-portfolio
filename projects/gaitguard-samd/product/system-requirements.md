## Requirement-writing checklist

A good requirement is:

- singular;
- unambiguous;
- measurable where possible;
- technically feasible;
- traceable to a user need or risk control;
- verifiable by an objective method.

Avoid words such as 'fast', 'easy' or 'accurate' unless you define the measurable acceptance criterion.
