## Versioning convention

- `0.x` - draft / development
- `1.0` - first approved portfolio baseline
- minor revision - clarification or low-impact change
- major revision - change that affects requirements, risk, architecture or verification evidence

## Review rule

When a controlled document changes, check whether linked requirements, risks, tests and traceability entries also need revision.
