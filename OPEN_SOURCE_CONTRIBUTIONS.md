## Why these contributions

I am prioritizing small, reviewable contributions to scientific Python projects that are close to my long-term interests in computational neuroscience, neural decoding, motor control, neurotechnology, and reproducible biomedical data analysis.

The goal is not to generate activity for its own sake. Each contribution should:

1. solve or clarify a real upstream issue;
2. include a reproducible explanation or test where appropriate;
3. remain small enough to review safely;
4. follow the project's existing conventions;
5. be submitted upstream only when the implementation is technically justified.
